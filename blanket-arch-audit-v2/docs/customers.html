A concrete, week-by-week guide to going from zero to 10 paying customers.

2. Week 1 — Identify 50 Prospects

Method A: LinkedIn Sales Navigator

  1. Search: "CTO" OR "VP Engineering" + industry: Healthcare IT, Fintech
  2. Filter: company size 20–200, funding stage Series A–C
  3. Save 50 profiles to a "BlanketAudit Prospects" list
  4. For each: note whether they handle PHI, PCI data, or mention SOC2 on their website

Method B: Crunchbase / AngelList

  1. Filter: Healthcare or Fintech, raised >$5M, founded after 2020
  2. Check careers page — open roles mentioning "security", "compliance", or "SOC2" = strong signal
  3. Cross-reference with LinkedIn to find the CTO/VP Eng

Method C: GitHub Reconnaissance

  1. Search GitHub for repos containing: "HIPAA", "patient", "phi", "card_number"
  2. Check if .env is committed (instant finding you can reference in outreach)
  3. Look for requirements.txt with outdated deps (CVE findings)
  4. Note: only scan public repos; this is for research, not uninvited scanning of private code

Method D: SOC2 Auditor Referrals

  1. Identify 5 SOC2 audit firms (Vanta partners, Drata partners, Secureframe partners)
  2. Email: "We offer free pre-audit code scans to your clients. Helps them pass faster = less rework for you."
  3. Offer a white-label version of the report with their branding
  4. Auditors refer 3–5 clients each → 15–25 warm leads
Output: Spreadsheet with 50 rows: Company, CTO name, LinkedIn URL, email, industry, trigger signal, notes.

7. After 10 Customers — Scale

  1. Build 3 case studies from your best customers. Format: "Company X went from Grade F to Grade B in 60 days. Here's how."
  2. Create ROI calculator on website: "Enter your team size and compliance target → see projected savings."
  3. Hire first sales rep to handle inbound demos (you should be doing product by now).
  4. Launch referral program: "$100 credit for every referred customer who signs up."
  5. Apply to Y Combinator / Techstars with: 10 customers, $3K MRR, 1,565 tests, working product.
  6. Raise pre-seed on the strength of: product built, customers paying, compliance domain expertise.

The Story You Tell Investors

"Every startup that wants enterprise customers needs SOC2. The prep takes 6 months and $200K in eng time. We compress it to 45 days with AI that reads code and maps it to compliance controls. We have 10 paying customers, $3K MRR, and the product is 36K lines of tested code with 1,565 passing tests. The market is every startup that handles sensitive data — that's 50,000+ companies."

8. The One Insight That Matters

Don't sell a "security scanner." Sell a "compliance accelerator."

CTOs don't wake up wanting another scanning tool. They wake up panicking about a SOC2 audit that's 4 months away and they haven't started. Your product turns "4 months of scrambling" into "here's a 45-day plan with exact tasks and effort estimates." That's what they'll pay $299/month for.

The report is the product. The AI findings are features. The compliance matrix is what closes deals. The score trend (31 → 52 → 76 → 88) is what prevents churn.
